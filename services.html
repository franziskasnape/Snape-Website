What I offer

Services

From structural repairs to scientific analysis, every treatment is tailored to the object and its condition.

Structural & Support Conservation

Tear mending, lining, and repairs to stretchers and supports to stabilise paintings for the long term.

Retouching & Colour Matching

Careful, reversible in-painting to reintegrate losses while remaining true to the artist's original palette.

Technical & Scientific Examination

Microscopy, cross-section and pigment analysis to understand materials, technique and condition before treatment begins.

Large-Scale & Decorative Works

Condition assessment and restoration of large decorative paintings, screens and theatrical scenery.
