The Conservator

About Me

I am a paintings and sculpture conservator-restorer, working with private collections, museums and heritage institutions. My approach combines hands-on treatment with careful technical examination — every intervention is documented, reversible where possible, and guided by respect for the original material and intent of the artist.

I am a proud member of SKR (Schweizerischer Verband für Konservierung und Restaurierung) and VDR (Verband der Restauratoren), and continue to build on years of hands-on experience across conservation studios, private commissions and cultural institutions.
