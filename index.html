Recent Work

Careful hands, scientific rigour

Every object carries its own history. I combine traditional craftsmanship with modern conservation science to stabilise, clean and restore paintings and sculptures — always respecting the integrity of the original work.

What I do

Structural Conservation

Tear mending, lining and support repairs that stabilise a work for the long term.

Retouching

Careful, reversible in-painting that stays true to the artist's original palette.

Technical Analysis

Microscopy and pigment analysis to understand material, technique and condition.
